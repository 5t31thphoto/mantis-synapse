SYNAPSE

A praying-mantis psychedelic fidget for M5Stack Core2

What it is

SYNAPSE turns the Core2 into a pocket organism: light, color, and motion driven by your hands, your voice, and the way you tilt the board. No menus to survive — just modes that feed on attention.

Built for the people who spin rings, click pens, and chase patterns. This one stares back.

How to play

  • Tilt — steer tunnels and wormholes, pull the swarm, bend the moiré, explore the fractal.
  • Touch — poke the eye (it hurts), pet the mantis, splash the pool, pop bubbles, drag through fractals, paint the synesthesia field.
  • Make noise — everything listens: spectrum, beats, and your voice drive color, shape, speed and the mantis's mouth.
  • Shake — hue jumps, the eye goes dizzy, the mantis gets woozy, palettes scramble.
  • A / C — previous / next mode · B — the mode's own variation.

Modes

SWARM
Living particle flock (flock / orbit / chaos). Touch to drop a tiny mantis in and scatter them.
EYE
A wet, blinking eyeball over hypnotic moiré: sound rings vs. tilt rings interfering. Poke it and it squirts tears. Shake it dizzy.
TUNNEL
B cycles: dive down a tube built from your sound; recede and sculpt the tube with tilt as it trails away; fractal morphing Julia worlds (tap for a new one); portal warp through wormholes into endless dimensions.
PULSE
Feedback visualizers that get stranger with each B: bloom, kaleido, spectrum star, phase space, synesthesia.
CALM
The physics room: glowing liquids, a splash pool, sand art between glass, a wave tank with a paper boat, and a blacklight aquarium. B changes room, long-press changes its mood.
MANTIS
A charming little mantis that dances to whatever it hears. B: sing mode, close-up, lip-syncing into a mic.

Calibrate the tilt

Lay the Core2 flat and hold a finger still in the middle of the screen. After 2 seconds a ring closes in; then it calibrates for a second ("hold flat - calibrating") and remembers it, even after a reboot.

Install on Core2

Chrome or Edge · USB data cable · board powered on.
