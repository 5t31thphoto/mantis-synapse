SYNAPSE

A praying-mantis psychedelic fidget for M5Stack Core2

What it is

SYNAPSE turns the Core2 into a pocket organism: light, color, and motion driven by your hands, your voice, and the way you tilt the board. No menus to survive — just modes that feed on attention.

Built for the people who spin rings, click pens, and chase patterns. This one stares back.

How to play

  • Tilt — gravity pulls the swarm; the compound eye tracks you; the tunnel banks.
  • Touch — paint trails, shove particles, stab the footer to change modes.
  • Make noise — mic level blooms color, thickens the pulse mandala, wakes the antennae.
  • Shake — hue jumps, particles explode, haptic kicks in.
  • A / C — previous / next mode · B — freeze-frame hold.

Modes

SWARM
Living particle flock with mantis antennae, audio breath, tilt gravity.
EYE
Compound-eye field; iris locked to IMU; occasional blink.
TUNNEL
Hex rings into infinity; spin with tilt; speed with volume.
PULSE
Radial mic mandala — speak or tap the table and watch it detonate.

Install on Core2

Chrome or Edge · USB data cable · board powered on.
